Controls
How to play
  • WASD Move your hero around the dungeon
  • N Set your gamertag
  • Click An agent to interact & rename them
  • T Open the chat box — it asks whichever agent you're next to
  • Space Open the activity log for the agent you're next to
  • Esc Close whatever panel is open
  • +− Zoom in and out (or scroll the wheel)
  • sound Toggle chimes (top-left corner)
